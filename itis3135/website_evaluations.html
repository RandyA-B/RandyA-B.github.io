CRAP Website Evaluations

Apple

  • Crap: Uses extreme contrast such as bold black or white text against high-resolution product photography, to create a clear focal point and make calls-to-action pop.
  • Repetition: Maintains a strict design language with consistent font styles, button shapes, and navigation layouts, making the site feel cohesive and professional.
  • Alignment: Employs a precise grid system where every element is perfectly centered or snapped to invisible lines, creating a sense of order and balance.
  • Proximity: Groups related items—like a product title, description, and Buy link—closely together with ample space between different sections to prevent confusion.

Bad HTML

  • Crap: Features weak or jarring contrast, such as dark text on rainbow backgrounds or vibrating neon colors, which strains the eyes and hides important information.
  • Repetition: Lacks any consistent theme; fonts, colors, and animation styles change randomly, resulting in a fragmented and disorganized experience.
  • Alignment: Items are scattered haphazardly across the screen; text is often unaligned or overlapping, giving the site a messy appearance.
  • Proximity: Related information is often separated by unrelated GIFs or ads, while unrelated elements are crammed together, making it difficult to understand the relationship between items.

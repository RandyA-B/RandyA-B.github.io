About the Chef

Ian Skinner is a UNCC student with a genuine passion for cooking and baking. He enjoys experimenting with flavors, trying new recipes, and sharing his creations with friends and classmates. Skinner's Recipes is his personal collection of favorite dishes, organized for anyone to explore and enjoy.

From hearty weeknight dinners to weekend baking projects, Ian approaches every recipe with curiosity and a willingness to improvise. His philosophy is simple: good food doesn't have to be complicated.

When he's not in the kitchen, Ian enjoys photography, hiking, and discovering new local restaurants around Charlotte.

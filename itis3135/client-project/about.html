About the Chef

Bob Ross is a UNCC student with a passion for cooking and baking. He enjoys experimenting with flavors, trying new recipes, and sharing his creations with friends and classmates. Ross’ Recipes is his personal collection of favorite dishes, organized for anyone to explore and enjoy.

When he’s not cooking, Bob enjoys photography, hiking, and discovering new local restaurants.
